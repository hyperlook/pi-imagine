# Style 2: Cinematic Photoshoot (电影质感写真)

### 完整英文 Prompt 模板
Turn my uploaded photo into a cinematic professional photoshoot. Place me in [SCENE_DESC] with dramatic but realistic lighting, natural shadows, shallow depth of field, subtle background blur, cinematic composition, realistic skin texture, detailed clothing, and professional color grading. Keep my identity and facial features consistent. The final image should look like an actual photograph from a high-end cinematic photoshoot.

### 默认场景（若用户未指定）
a cinematic moody scene with atmospheric lighting, cinematic composition, and rich color depth
